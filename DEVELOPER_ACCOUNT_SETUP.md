## Part 1: Google Play Developer Account (45 min)

### Step 1: Visit Google Play Console

1. Go to: **https://play.google.com/console/**
2. Sign in with your Google account
   - If you don't have one, create at: https://accounts.google.com/SignUp
   - Use: pitsephali@gmail.com (or your email)
   - Create password and enable 2FA

### Step 2: Create Developer Account

1. **Click "Create account"** (or "Get started")
2. **Fill Developer Information:**
   - **Developer name:** Pits Marketing and Distribution
   - **Email address:** pitsephali@gmail.com
   - **Phone number:** Your phone number (for support)
   - **Address:** Your business address

3. **Accept Developer Program Policies**
   - Read the terms
   - Check: "I agree to..."
   - Click "Accept and continue"

### Step 3: Pay Registration Fee

1. **Price:** $25 USD (one-time)
2. **Payment method:**
   - Debit or credit card
   - Google Pay
   - PayPal (sometimes)
3. **Click "Pay $25"**
4. **Enter payment details:**
   - Card number
   - Expiration date
   - CVV
   - Billing address
5. **Confirm payment**

### Step 4: Account Activation

**Status:** 
- Payment processed: Immediate (usually)
- Account active: Immediate to 24 hours
- Can start publishing: Once active

**What happens next:**
- You'll receive confirmation email
- Google Play Console dashboard opens
- "Create app" button appears

### Step 5: Verify Account Setup

1. **Go to:** https://play.google.com/console/
2. **You should see:**
   - "Your apps" section
   - "Create app" button
   - Developer settings (click ☰ → Settings)

3. **In Settings, verify:**
   - Developer name: "Pits Marketing and Distribution" ✓
   - Email: pitsephali@gmail.com ✓

**✅ Google Play account is ready!**

## Troubleshooting

### Google Play Payment Failed

**Problem:** Card declined, payment fails

**Solutions:**
1. Verify card has international payments enabled
2. Try different payment method
3. Contact your bank (they may block $25 charge)
4. Try again after 24 hours
5. Contact Google Play support

### Huawei Email Verification Not Received

**Problem:** Verification email doesn't arrive

**Solutions:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 15 minutes and refresh
3. Click "Resend verification email"
4. Try another email address
5. Contact Huawei support

### Can't Access Developer Console

**Problem:** Says "Account not ready" or "Pending review"

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 24 hours for activation
2. Check email for any pending actions
3. Verify all profile information is complete
4. Contact support in each platform
