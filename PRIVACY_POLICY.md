# Privacy Policy for STUAPS

**Effective Date:** August 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

STUAPS ("**we**," "**us**," "**our**," or "**Company**") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application ("**App**") and our website at stuaps.vercel.app (collectively, the "**Service**").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration & Authentication:**
- Email address, username, and password hash
- Full name, contact information
- Tenant/organization details
- Role and permission level

**Business Operations Data:**
- Student information (names, student numbers, surnames, contact details)
- Invoice data (invoice numbers, dates, amounts, line items)
- Property and institution information
- Pest control treatment records
- Payment and billing information
- Payroll information
- Financial statements and reconciliation data

**Communication:**
- Support inquiries, feedback, and bug reports
- Email communications regarding your account

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Session & Device Data:**
- Session cookies and authentication tokens
- IP address and browser type
- Device information and operating system
- Approximate geographic location (country/region level)
- Login timestamps and access patterns

**Usage Analytics:**
- Pages and features accessed
- Time spent on features
- Actions performed (without logging specific data values)
- Error and crash reports

- **Email Service:** For transactional communications

These providers are contractually obligated to:
- Use data only for providing the requested service
- Maintain confidentiality and security
- Comply with data protection laws

**Legal Requirements:**
We may disclose information when required by law:
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Government requests

- Enforcing our Terms of Service

### 4.2 Data We Do NOT Share

- Student personal details are not shared with external marketing companies
- Your billing information is never sold or shared for promotional purposes

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

**Active Account Data:**
- Retained for the duration of your subscription
- Includes all students, invoices, properties, and operational records

**Account Closure:**
- Upon deletion request, user data is securely deleted within 30 days
- Aggregate, anonymized data may be retained for analytics
- Backup copies may take up to 90 days to fully expire

**Legal/Compliance Data:**
- Transaction records retained per South African tax requirements (7 years)
- Access logs retained for 1 year
- Security incident logs retained for investigation purposes

**Automatic Deletion:**
- Session cookies deleted upon logout or browser close
- Temporary tokens expire after their designated timeframe

### 5.2 Data You Can Manage

You may:
- Update or correct your personal information anytime in account settings
- Export your data in common formats
- Request permanent deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Contact support@stuaps.com for data requests

## 6. Security of Your Information

### 6.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

**In Transit:**
- All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Secure socket layer (SSL) certificates
- API request authentication and validation

**At Rest:**
- Password hashing using bcrypt with salts
- Encrypted database connections
- Secure credential storage (no hardcoded secrets)
- Regular security patches and updates

**Access Control:**
- Role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Viewer)
- Session management with secure cookies (HttpOnly, SameSite)
- Multi-tenant isolation (tenant data never cross-accessible)
- Login attempt rate limiting

### 6.2 Limitations

While we use reasonable security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security against all attacks. You are responsible for:
- Keeping your password confidential
- Logging out from shared devices
- Reporting suspicious account activity immediately
